**First change to ship:** Put **Record usage** directly on a stocked shelf row. Today, a worker looking at House espresso and its shelf must open **Update**, find **Record usage**, and only then enter the quantity. The existing form already receives the correct shelf. Removing the action menu cuts this path from two actions to one before entry, without changing stock accounting.

## Evidence and design language

Reviewed November 12, 2026 against commit `bab50df` plus the working-tree inventory, location and responsive layout changes. The audited product is the React/Spring Boot app. Tests used fresh Alder Coffee businesses, real owner and invited employee accounts, and separate browser sessions. The shared Juniper Coffee workspace was left available. The findings describe current behavior. The redesign and its acceptance criteria are future work.

The live interface uses neutral surfaces, teal actions and selection, tabular stock quantities, a desktop sidebar, five phone tabs, and shared dialogs that become sheets on phones. The governing components are WorkspaceLayout, PageHeader, Field, Dialog and the stock forms. The existing phone quantity column, role-filtered actions and shelf-first contents are useful foundations. Explicit exceptions: none documented for the reviewed flows. The review used the UI Skills improve-ui evidence checks, baseline-ui and fixing-accessibility guidance, and Backroom’s own component contracts. ## Findings

**Priority** reflects the task interrupted. P1 affects routine stock work or decisions. P2 affects repeat navigation, investigation or setup. Confidence applies to the observed behavior. How much time each change saves a real team still needs user testing.

## What worked and should survive the redesign

- The low-stock card prefilled item, shelf and shortfall. An existing open request linked directly to its record. On request detail, **Requested**, **Approved**, **Received** and **Still expected** correctly showed 5 kg, 4 kg, 1.5 kg and 2.5 kg. These are good patterns for carrying context and showing units. [Partial delivery](evidence/partial-delivery.png)
- The real employee received a manager-directed explanation for an unlinked barcode. **Update** targets measured 44 CSS px high on the phone. Tab stayed in the tested sheet, Escape closed it, and focus returned to the originating row after both the action sheet and the subsequent usage form.
- Invalid stock removal kept the quantity form open with an alert. An injected inventory read failure offered **Try again** and recovered after connectivity returned. A delayed inventory read exposed a loading status. An unmatched search offered **Clear search**. Direct employee access to Activity showed the role restriction.

## Verification and limits

Both roles visited Home, Inventory, item detail, shelf detail, Scan, Requests and request detail at 390 × 844, 1280 × 800 and 320 × 844. No document-level horizontal overflow was measured across those 42 route and viewport combinations. Owner inventory also passed a 640 × 400 reflow check and a 200% CSS zoom spot check. These checks do not cover every dialog or native browser zoom setting.

All six existing Playwright scenarios passed against the current frontend using Edge, including invitation acceptance, employee restrictions, reset links, stock movements, restocking with two deliveries, and QR sign-in. The camera test used a synthetic device. The additional role walkthroughs exercised real transactions and captured the screenshots above. First-time comprehension, physical camera performance, screen-reader usability and speed under shift pressure still require observation with people. [Browser scenarios](../frontend/e2e/)
