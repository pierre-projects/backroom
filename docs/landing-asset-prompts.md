# Landing page pictures: Gemini prompts

Pictures 1 to 4 are done and on the page. **3 to go.**

## How it works

1. **Copy a prompt** below and paste it into Gemini.
2. **Check the result** against the list under the prompt. If something's wrong, ask Gemini again with the fix in brackets after it. Don't spend long on lines, seams or blurry patches: I can fix those.
3. **Send the picture to me.** I put the real app screen (or QR label) on the blank screen, so every word is correct.

Gemini only makes the photo. It never draws the app, because it scrambles the words.

**Every prompt asks for the same things:**

- The phone or laptop screen is **completely black** and faces the camera **straight on**, not at an angle. That's what lets me paste the real screen in exactly.
- The device is **big**: on the website, pictures show at about half size, and a small phone becomes a speck.
- **One continuous scene**, gradually softer toward the back, not split into a sharp half and a blurry half.
- **No logos, brand names or readable text** on any product, box, bottle or sign.
